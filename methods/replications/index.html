Replications

Checked against the textbooks.

Well-known models from the literature, rebuilt in Latris, with the results each is known to give. Each expected value says where it comes from: a number printed in the original, a formula the model satisfies exactly, or an exact calculation from the published inputs done without Latris. They ship with the library, so you can rerun every one: pip install latris, then latris replicate check.

Latris 0.20.0: 5 of 5 replications reproduce every expected result.

Bass diffusion of a new product Reproduced

SD · Bass, F. M. (1969). A new product growth for model consumer durables. Management Science, 15(5), 215–227.

Where each expected value comes from
  • Adopters after 5 years: N(t) = M (1 − e^(−(p+q)t)) / (1 + (q/p) e^(−(p+q)t)), with p = 0.03, q = 0.38, M = 1,000,000.
  • Adopters after 10 years: N(t) = M (1 − e^(−(p+q)t)) / (1 + (q/p) e^(−(p+q)t)), with p = 0.03, q = 0.38, M = 1,000,000.
  • Adopters after 20 years: N(t) = M (1 − e^(−(p+q)t)) / (1 + (q/p) e^(−(p+q)t)), with p = 0.03, q = 0.38, M = 1,000,000.
  • When adoptions peak (years): t* = ln(q/p) / (p + q).
  • Adoptions a year at the peak: dN/dt at t* = M (p + q)² / (4q).

Kermack–McKendrick SIR epidemic Reproduced

SD · Kermack, W. O. & McKendrick, A. G. (1927). A contribution to the mathematical theory of epidemics. Proceedings of the Royal Society of London, Series A, 115(772), 700–721.

CheckExpectedLatris Basis
People ever infected, once the epidemic is over (day 300)796816796816Closed formReproduced
Most people infectious at once153431153449Closed formReproduced
Where each expected value comes from
  • People ever infected, once the epidemic is over (day 300): The final-size relation ln(s∞ / s₀) = R₀ (s∞ − s₀ − i₀), with R₀ = β/γ = 2, s₀ = 0.99999, i₀ = 0.00001; ever infected = N (1 − s∞).
  • Most people infectious at once: i_max = i₀ + s₀ − (1 + ln(R₀ s₀)) / R₀, from the conserved quantity s + i − ln(s)/R₀.
