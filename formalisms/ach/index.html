The methods, their references and their limits

Analysis of competing hypotheses

Used for: every evidence item rated against every hypothesis (CC to II) with its reasoning; hypotheses ranked by credibility- and relevance-weighted inconsistency, the least evidence against, not the most for; evidence that doesn't discriminate; linchpin items without which the lead changes or ties, with whether their sources were checked.

Limits: Ratings and weights are judgements; the weights (high 1.0, medium 0.7, low 0.4; an inconsistency 1, a strong one 2) are Latris's stated convention, not Heuer's; items are treated as independent and no probabilities are computed, the ranking orders hypotheses, it doesn't say how likely each is.

References: Heuer (1999), Psychology of Intelligence Analysis, ch. 8; Heuer & Pherson (2015), Structured Analytic Techniques for Intelligence Analysis

Every number carries where it came from, and a value nobody has verified is flagged where the result rests on it. Every method Latris implements is in the methods reference.
