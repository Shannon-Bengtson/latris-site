The methods, their references and their limits

Multi-criteria decision analysis (additive value model)

Used for: options' scores turned into 0–1 values on linear scales, combined by normalised swing weights; the ranking with each criterion's contribution, dominated options, and for each weight the nearest values (others keeping their proportions) at which the top choice changes.

Limits: Additive and linear: assumes the criteria are preferentially independent and value is linear between worst and best; weights are judgements and mean nothing apart from their scales; scales taken from the options' range change when an option is added; one-way weight sensitivity only, no probabilistic weights or scores, and no AHP or outranking methods.

References: Keeney & Raiffa (1976), Decisions with Multiple Objectives; Belton & Stewart (2002), Multiple Criteria Decision Analysis; UK DCLG (2009), Multi-criteria analysis: a manual

Every number carries where it came from, and a value nobody has verified is flagged where the result rests on it. Every method Latris implements is in the methods reference.
