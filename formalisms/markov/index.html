The methods, their references and their limits

Cohort Markov model and incremental cost-effectiveness analysis

Used for: a cohort moving between states each cycle (per-strategy transition probabilities, the rest staying); discounted costs and outcomes with optional half-cycle correction; incremental analysis, dominance, extended dominance, ICERs along the frontier, and net monetary benefit at a willingness to pay.

Limits: Memoryless: the chance of moving depends only on the current state, not on time in it (no tunnel states or time-varying probabilities yet); point estimates only, no probabilistic sensitivity analysis or acceptability curves yet; a cohort expectation, not individual-level simulation.

References: Sonnenberg & Beck (1993), "Markov Models in Medical Decision Making", Medical Decision Making 13(4); Briggs, Claxton & Sculpher (2006), Decision Modelling for Health Economic Evaluation; Drummond et al. (2015), Methods for the Economic Evaluation of Health Care Programmes, 4th ed.
