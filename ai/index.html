Use with AI

Use Latris from the AI you already use.

Ask Claude or ChatGPT a causal question and it builds the model in Latris: factors, links with their reasons, and sources with the passage it read. You get a report link you can send as it is.

The AI does the drafting. Latris keeps it honest: everything the AI adds is labelled AI-proposed, and its evidence stays unverified until a person checks it.

In a chat app

Add the connector, then ask.

The connector address:

https://mcp.latris.app/mcp
1

Add Latris as a connector

In Claude, add a custom connector. In ChatGPT, add an app in developer mode. Use the address above. No sign-in needed.

2

Ask a causal question

For example: "Use Latris to map what drives volunteer burnout." It may first ask up to three short questions: the background and the decision, who will read it, and what you expect the answer to be.

3

Get the report link

The chat hands you a link to the interactive report. Send it as it is: anyone can open it, no account needed.

4

Save it to keep it

Until it's saved, a model lasts 30 days after its last change. Ask the AI to "save this model", open the one-time link, and sign in with your email. Saved models get a link that doesn't expire and open in the full editor.

What stays honest

The report says what the AI did.

Factors, links, evidence and prose the AI adds are recorded as AI-proposed. Sources it cites are unverified until someone reads them, and the report says "not yet checked against the source" until then.

When the AI says a source supports a link, it has to quote the passage, and Latris looks the quote up in the text it saved for that source. A paraphrase is refused. A study it only read about in another source is marked as read second-hand.

None of that makes the model right. It makes it checkable, which is what a reviewer needs. What reviewers see.

Agents and the terminal

Claude Code, Codex, or any agent with a shell.

The open-source engine is a Python CLI built to be driven by an agent. It needs no API key: the agent you're already using does the AI work, and every step stays on the record.

pip install "latris[data,quant]"

Agents start at llms.txt, which maps the documentation and the working rules. With no network, latris docs prints the same guide from the installed package. latris link turns a model into a report link without any network access, so it works from a sandbox.

Your data

What Latris receives.

Through the connector, Latris receives only the model content the chat app sends it. It doesn't receive your name, your email or your chat history; your conversation stays with the chat app's provider under its own terms. Latris doesn't send your models to AI providers. Model data is stored in Sydney, Australia.

Details are in the Privacy Notice.
