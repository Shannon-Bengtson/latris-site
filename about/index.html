About

I'm Shannon Bengtson. I build Latris, and I use it.

I work on causal modelling in the climate and environment space, where decision-makers need to be able to trust and act on the information I provide. Lately I'm watching the industry run at two speeds: the traditional, methodical, carefully researched approach, which no longer fits tighter timelines and rising demand, and generic, high-level, indefensible output from LLMs, produced en masse.

I'm a climate scientist by training (PhD, UNSW; first-class honours in chemical and environmental engineering, UQ), and I've spent years building models that had to survive that scrutiny: emissions and physical-risk analytics audited against regulatory standards, scenario analysis for climate disclosure, technical teams across government science, consulting and climate-tech.

Latris is my attempt to fix that disconnect. The idea at its core is to bring traceability to every part of the modelling process, in a formal modelling workbench. That unblocks people to use AI while maintaining their integrity, and gives end-users all the information behind the answer.
